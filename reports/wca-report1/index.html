01 // THE FINDING
What the Analysis Produced

Fragmentation of the security apparatus correlates with conflict intensity at r = +0.81. State legitimacy deficit does not, and its apparent association disappears once fragmentation is held constant. Two clusters of states share an identical mean legitimacy deficit of 8.74 and differ by 3.54 points of conflict intensity.

This revises the framing I had used in earlier work on the same region, where structural governance failure led the causal account. The data does not support that ordering. Publishing the revision, with the falsification tests that would overturn it, is the point of the portfolio.
