CAMEROON + CHAD // 2020 TO AUG 2026

AI-Assisted Investigation, Governed by the evidence

This product publishes the human-in-the-loop audit trail rather than presenting machine assistance as authority. The worked question is whether Cameroon's rising incident burden reflects a real shift toward coercive control or an artifact that the current data cannot distinguish.

Scope boundary. The primary comparison uses 34 matched weekly observations: 4 Jan to 23 Aug 2025 and 3 Jan to 29 Aug 2026. The workbook contains weekly aggregated category-geography records, not raw narrative event reports. Population exposure is missing in 2,129 of 7,598 rows and is not imputed or used in the core statistical battery. All real-world events after 29 Aug 2026 post-date this evidence base.

01 // BLUF

Anomaly before explanation

Anomaly

Cameroon events rose 32.5% while fatalities rose 11.6%, and abduction rose 55.2%.

Best-supported reading

The data are consistent with a shift toward coercive activity, but they do not discriminate cleanly among intent, coding, fragmentation and criminal opportunism.

03 // AUDIT TRAIL

Accepted, modified and rejected proposals

MODIFY
A1 // Frame the central finding

Model output: Initial draft emphasized highest-risk regions.

Analyst ruling: Reframed around divergence between event composition and mortality because the workflow prioritizes divergence over ranking.

REJECT
A2 // Define the year-on-year window

Model output: Initial extraction included the 30 Aug 2025 week.

Analyst ruling: It produced a 35-week comparison. The matched comparison was corrected to 34 weeks ending 23 Aug 2025 and 29 Aug 2026.

REJECT
A3 // Interpret coercive share and lethality

Model output: A causal sentence linking coercion to lower lethality was proposed.

Analyst ruling: Cross-sectional ADMIN1 data cannot establish direction. The product reports association and names reverse causation.

ACCEPT WITH CAVEAT
A4 // Retain the k-means partition

Model output: A one-member cluster looked visually awkward.

Analyst ruling: Computed membership was retained and the one-member cluster is disclosed as unstable rather than silently reassigned.

REJECT
A5 // Use population exposure as a denominator

Model output: Exposure could have produced attractive rates.

Analyst ruling: 2,129 rows are missing population exposure. No imputation is permitted, so the field is excluded from the core battery.

08 // CONFIDENCE

Moderate confidence in the descriptive finding

Overall confidence: Moderate. The event-composition divergence is directly measured and internally consistent, the matched windows are verified, and the principal category totals reproduce deterministically. Confidence does not rise to high because the workbook lacks actor attribution, event narratives, denominator completeness and coding-method metadata needed to adjudicate causal explanations.
