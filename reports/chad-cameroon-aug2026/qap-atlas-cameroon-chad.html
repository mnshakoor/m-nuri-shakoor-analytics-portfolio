04 // LIMITS

Centroids are not boundaries

Aggregation

ADMIN1 centroids conceal within-region variation and route-level exposure.

Scale

Bubble size is relative within each layer and should not be compared across layers.

Politics

The display is analytical. It carries no statement on disputed boundaries or territorial status.
