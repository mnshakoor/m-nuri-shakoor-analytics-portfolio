02 / Method

The Quanta Analytica Process™

QAP is a structured workflow for moving from an uncertain question to a traceable analytic judgment. It integrates evidence discipline, formal analytic techniques, calibrated confidence, and reproducible production.

Architecture before narrative. Structured reasoning precedes generative output.
  1. 01

    Source discipline

    Web-sourced information is assessed for reliability, credibility, relevance, and corroboration.

  2. 02

    Evidence traceability

    Substantive claims connect to cited evidence, clearly labeled inference, or explicit assumptions.

  3. 03

    Calibrated confidence

    Confidence language remains proportionate to the quality, consistency, and coverage of available evidence.

  4. 04

    Structured analytic techniques

    Formal methods test assumptions, competing explanations, causal mechanisms, indicators, and scenarios.

  5. 05

    Reproducibility

    Data preparation, methodology, sources, and limitations are documented at the product level.
